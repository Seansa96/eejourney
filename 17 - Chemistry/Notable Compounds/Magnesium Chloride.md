$MgCl_2$

Formed through a combination reaction with the metal [[Magnesium]], heated in the presence of [[Chlorine]] gas.  

This forms a metallic salt that has positive Magnesium ions and negative Chlorine ions.  It's highly soluble and widely known for its hygroscopicity.  It is typically used for de-icing, dust control, and as a food coagulant.

Aqueous solutions involving it are generally acidic to neutral, and it is a key industrial precursor for magnesium metal.

It usually has a white or colorless appearance and is corrosive to metals.
