## Moles calculations

$$n=\frac{m}{M},\qquad m=nM,\qquad M=\frac{m}{n}$$

where $n$ is amount in moles, $m$ is mass, and $M$ is molar mass in $\mathrm{g/mol}$.

$$N=nN_A,\qquad n=\frac{N}{N_A},\qquad N_A=6.02214076\times10^{23}\ \mathrm{mol^{-1}}$$

For atoms, molecules, ions, or formula units, $N$ is the number of those entities. Use the balanced equation's coefficients as mole ratios:

$$n_{\mathrm{wanted}}=n_{\mathrm{given}}\left(\frac{\text{coefficient of wanted substance}}{\text{coefficient of given substance}}\right)$$

## Concentration

$$M=\frac{n}{V},\qquad m=nM,\qquad C_1V_1=C_2V_2$$

| Quantity | Conversion / definition |
|---|---|
| molarity | $\mathrm M=\mathrm{mol/L}$ |
| molality | $\mathrm m=\mathrm{mol/kg\ solvent}$ |
| mass percent | $\%\,m/m=\frac{m_{solute}}{m_{solution}}\times100$ |
| volume percent | $\%\,v/v=\frac{V_{solute}}{V_{solution}}\times100$ |
| parts per million | $1\ \mathrm{ppm}=10^{-6}$; for dilute water, $\approx1\ \mathrm{mg/L}$ |
| parts per billion | $1\ \mathrm{ppb}=10^{-9}$; for dilute water, $\approx1\ \mathrm{\mu g/L}$ |

## Gas laws

$$PV=nRT,\qquad R=0.082057\ \mathrm{L\,atm\,mol^{-1}\,K^{-1}}$$

$$R=8.314462618\ \mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}\,K^{-1}}$$

$$T(\mathrm K)=T(^\circ\mathrm C)+273.15$$

At STP ($1\ \mathrm{atm}$ and $0^\circ\mathrm C$), an ideal gas occupies approximately $22.414\ \mathrm{L/mol}$.

## Pressure-volume and compound units

Pressure times volume has units of energy:

$$\mathrm{Pa\cdot m^3}=\left(\frac{\mathrm N}{\mathrm{m^2}}\right)\mathrm{m^3}=\mathrm{N\cdot m}=\mathrm J$$

Useful shortcuts:

$$1\ \mathrm{kPa\cdot L}=1\ \mathrm J,\qquad1\ \mathrm{L\cdot atm}=101.325\ \mathrm J$$

$$1\ \mathrm{bar\cdot L}=100\ \mathrm J,\qquad1\ \mathrm{mL}=1\ \mathrm{cm^3}=10^{-6}\ \mathrm{m^3}$$

For cubic length units, apply the prefix to all three dimensions:

$$1\ \mathrm{mm^3}=(10^{-3}\ \mathrm m)^3=10^{-9}\ \mathrm{m^3}$$

$$1\ \mathrm{cm^3}=10^{-6}\ \mathrm{m^3},\qquad1\ \mathrm{dm^3}=1\ \mathrm L=10^{-3}\ \mathrm{m^3}$$

### Example: pressure-volume rate

$$14.4\ \mathrm{kPa\cdot mm^3\,s^{-1}}
=14.4\left(10^3\ \mathrm{Pa}\right)\left(10^{-9}\ \mathrm{m^3}\right)\mathrm{s^{-1}}$$

$$=14.4\times10^{-6}\ \mathrm{J\,s^{-1}}
=0.0144\ \mathrm{mJ\,s^{-1}}$$

## Energy

$$q=mc\Delta T,\qquad1\ \mathrm{cal}=4.184\ \mathrm J$$

$$1\ \mathrm{kcal}=4184\ \mathrm J,\qquad1\ \mathrm{kJ}=0.239006\ \mathrm{kcal}$$

$$\Delta G=\Delta H-T\Delta S$$

## Pressure

$$1\ \mathrm{atm}=101.325\ \mathrm{kPa}=760\ \mathrm{mmHg}=760\ \mathrm{torr}=1.01325\ \mathrm{bar}$$

$$1\ \mathrm{psi}=6.89476\ \mathrm{kPa}$$

## Solution preparation

$$\text{mass needed}=M_{target}\times V_{final}\times M_{molar}$$

For a mass/volume solution:

$$\%\,m/v=\frac{\mathrm{g\ solute}}{\mathrm{mL\ solution}}\times100$$

## Atomic and spectroscopic units

$$1\ \mathrm u=1.66053906660\times10^{-27}\ \mathrm{kg}$$

$$E=h\nu=\frac{hc}{\lambda},\qquad c=2.99792458\times10^8\ \mathrm{m/s}$$

$$1\ \mathrm{eV}=1.602176634\times10^{-19}\ \mathrm J$$

$$\tilde\nu=\frac1\lambda\quad\text{(wavenumber, commonly in }\mathrm{cm^{-1}}\text{)}$$
