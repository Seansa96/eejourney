## Time

$$1\ \mathrm{min}=60\ \mathrm{s},\quad1\ \mathrm{h}=3600\ \mathrm{s},\quad1\ \mathrm{day}=86400\ \mathrm{s}.$$

$$1\ \mathrm{week}=7\ \mathrm{days},\quad1\ \mathrm{common\ year}=365\ \mathrm{days}.$$

## Temperature

$$T_{\mathrm K}=T_{\mathrm{°C}}+273.15$$

$$T_{\mathrm{°C}}=\frac{5}{9}(T_{\mathrm{°F}}-32),\qquad T_{\mathrm{°F}}=\frac{9}{5}T_{\mathrm{°C}}+32$$

$$T_{\mathrm{°R}}=T_{\mathrm{°F}}+459.67,\qquad T_{\mathrm{°C}}=T_{\mathrm K}-273.15$$

## Force, pressure, energy, and power

| Quantity | Useful equalities |
|---|---|
| Force | $1\ \mathrm{lbf}=4.44822\ \mathrm N$; $1\ \mathrm N=1\ \mathrm{kg\,m/s^2}$ |
| Pressure | $1\ \mathrm{atm}=101325\ \mathrm{Pa}=760\ \mathrm{mmHg}$; $1\ \mathrm{bar}=10^5\ \mathrm{Pa}$; $1\ \mathrm{psi}=6894.76\ \mathrm{Pa}$ |
| Energy | $1\ \mathrm J=1\ \mathrm{N\,m}$; $1\ \mathrm{cal}=4.184\ \mathrm J$; $1\ \mathrm{kWh}=3.6\times10^6\ \mathrm J$; $1\ \mathrm{BTU}\approx1055.06\ \mathrm J$ |
| Power | $1\ \mathrm W=1\ \mathrm{J/s}$; $1\ \mathrm{hp}\approx745.7\ \mathrm W$ |
