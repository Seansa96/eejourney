## Medication and fluid calculations

$$\text{dose (mg)}=\text{ordered dose (mg/kg)}\times\text{mass (kg)}$$

$$\text{volume to administer}=\frac{\text{dose ordered}}{\text{dose on hand}}\times\text{volume on hand}$$

$$\text{flow rate (mL/h)}=\frac{\text{total volume (mL)}}{\text{time (h)}}$$

$$\text{flow rate (gtt/min)}=\frac{\text{volume (mL)}\times\text{drop factor (gtt/mL)}}{\text{time (min)}}$$

## Concentration

$$\%\,m/v=\frac{\mathrm{g\ solute}}{100\ \mathrm{mL\ solution}}$$

Therefore, a $1\%\,m/v$ solution contains $1\ \mathrm g/100\ \mathrm{mL}=10\ \mathrm{mg/mL}$.

$$1\ \mathrm{mg/mL}=1000\ \mathrm{\mu g/mL}$$

## Temperature

$$T_{\mathrm{°C}}=\frac59(T_{\mathrm{°F}}-32),\qquad T_{\mathrm{°F}}=\frac95T_{\mathrm{°C}}+32$$

$$T_{\mathrm K}=T_{\mathrm{°C}}+273.15$$

## Body mass and height

$$\text{kg}=\frac{\text{lb}}{2.20462},\qquad\text{cm}=2.54\times\text{inches}$$

$$\mathrm{BMI}=\frac{\text{mass (kg)}}{[\text{height (m)}]^2}$$

## Flow and pressure

$$1\ \mathrm{L/min}=1000\ \mathrm{mL/min}$$

$$1\ \mathrm{mmHg}=0.133322\ \mathrm{kPa},\qquad1\ \mathrm{kPa}=7.50062\ \mathrm{mmHg}$$

$$1\ \mathrm{atm}=760\ \mathrm{mmHg}=101.325\ \mathrm{kPa}$$
