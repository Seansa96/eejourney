## Angles

$$180^\circ=\pi\ \mathrm{rad},\qquad1^\circ=\frac{\pi}{180}\ \mathrm{rad},\qquad1\ \mathrm{rad}=\frac{180}{\pi}^\circ$$

$$1\ \mathrm{revolution}=360^\circ=2\pi\ \mathrm{rad},\qquad1\ \mathrm{arcsecond}=\frac{1}{3600}^\circ$$

## Coordinates

### Polar and Cartesian

$$x=r\cos\theta,\qquad y=r\sin\theta$$

$$r=\sqrt{x^2+y^2},\qquad\theta=\operatorname{atan2}(y,x)$$

### Cylindrical and Cartesian

$$x=r\cos\theta,\quad y=r\sin\theta,\quad z=z,\quad r=\sqrt{x^2+y^2}$$

### Spherical and Cartesian

Using $\rho$ for distance from the origin and $\phi$ for the angle from the positive $z$-axis:

$$x=\rho\sin\phi\cos\theta,\quad y=\rho\sin\phi\sin\theta,\quad z=\rho\cos\phi$$

$$\rho=\sqrt{x^2+y^2+z^2},\qquad r=\rho\sin\phi$$

## Complex numbers

$$z=a+bi=r(\cos\theta+i\sin\theta)=re^{i\theta}$$

$$r=|z|=\sqrt{a^2+b^2},\qquad\theta=\arg z$$

$$e^{i\theta}=\cos\theta+i\sin\theta$$

## Logarithms and exponentials

$$\log_b x=\frac{\ln x}{\ln b},\qquad\ln x=\log_e x$$

$$\log_{10}x=\frac{\ln x}{\ln10},\qquad10^x=e^{x\ln10}$$

## Geometry

| Shape | Area / volume |
|---|---|
| Circle | $A=\pi r^2$, $C=2\pi r$ |
| Sphere | $A=4\pi r^2$, $V=\frac43\pi r^3$ |
| Cylinder | $V=\pi r^2h$ |
| Cone | $V=\frac13\pi r^2h$ |
| Rectangular prism | $V=lwh$ |
| Triangle | $A=\frac12bh$ |

## Dimensional analysis

Treat a conversion factor as a fraction equal to one, oriented to cancel the unwanted unit:

$$25\ \mathrm{mi}\left(\frac{1.609344\ \mathrm{km}}{1\ \mathrm{mi}}\right)=40.2336\ \mathrm{km}$$

For squared or cubed quantities, square or cube the entire conversion factor:

$$1\ \mathrm{ft^2}\left(\frac{0.3048\ \mathrm m}{1\ \mathrm{ft}}\right)^2=0.092903\ \mathrm{m^2}$$
