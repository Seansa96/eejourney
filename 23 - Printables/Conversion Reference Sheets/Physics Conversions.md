## Mechanics

$$1\ \mathrm N=1\ \mathrm{kg\,m/s^2},\qquad1\ \mathrm{dyne}=10^{-5}\ \mathrm N$$

$$1\ \mathrm J=1\ \mathrm{N\,m}=1\ \mathrm{kg\,m^2/s^2}$$

$$1\ \mathrm{W}=1\ \mathrm{J/s},\qquad1\ \mathrm{hp}\approx745.7\ \mathrm W$$

$$1\ \mathrm{slug}=14.5939\ \mathrm{kg},\qquad1\ \mathrm{lbf}=4.44822\ \mathrm N$$

## Thermal physics

$$1\ \mathrm{BTU}\approx1055.06\ \mathrm J,\qquad1\ \mathrm{therm}=100000\ \mathrm{BTU}$$

$$1\ \mathrm{kWh}=3.6\ \mathrm{MJ},\qquad1\ \mathrm{cal}=4.184\ \mathrm J$$

$$T_{\mathrm K}=T_{\mathrm{°C}}+273.15$$

## Waves and optics

## Electricity and magnetism

$$1\ \mathrm C=1\ \mathrm{A\,s},\quad1\ \mathrm V=1\ \mathrm{J/C},\quad1\ \mathrm{T}=1\ \mathrm{Wb/m^2}$$

$$1\ \mathrm{G}=10^{-4}\ \mathrm T,\qquad1\ \mathrm{Wb}=1\ \mathrm{V\,s}$$

$$1\ \mathrm{F}=1\ \mathrm{C/V},\qquad1\ \mathrm H=1\ \mathrm{V\,s/A}$$

$$
1 \ohm = \frac{V}{A}, \qquad 1 \ohm = \frac{W}{A^2}
$$

## Atomic and nuclear physics

$$1\ \mathrm{eV}=1.602176634\times10^{-19}\ \mathrm J$$

$$1\ \mathrm{MeV}=10^6\ \mathrm{eV},\qquad1\ \mathrm u\,c^2=931.494\ \mathrm{MeV}$$

$$1\ \mathrm{Bq}=1\ \mathrm{decay/s},\qquad1\ \mathrm{Ci}=3.7\times10^{10}\ \mathrm{Bq}$$

$$1\ \mathrm{Gy}=1\ \mathrm{J/kg},\qquad1\ \mathrm{Sv}=1\ \mathrm{J/kg}\text{ weighted for biological effect}$$
