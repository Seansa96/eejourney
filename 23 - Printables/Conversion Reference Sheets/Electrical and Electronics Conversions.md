## Core electrical quantities

$$Q=It,\qquad V=\frac{W}{Q},\qquad P=VI$$

| Unit | Equivalent |
|---|---|
| coulomb | $1\ \mathrm C=1\ \mathrm{A\,s}$ |
| volt | $1\ \mathrm V=1\ \mathrm{J/C}$ |
| watt | $1\ \mathrm W=1\ \mathrm{V\,A}$ |
| watt-hour | $1\ \mathrm{Wh}=3600\ \mathrm J$ |
| kilowatt-hour | $1\ \mathrm{kWh}=3.6\ \mathrm{MJ}$ |

## Resistance, conductance, and power

$$V=IR,\qquad R=\frac{V}{I},\qquad G=\frac1R$$

$$P=VI=I^2R=\frac{V^2}{R}$$

$$1\ \mathrm{S}=1\ \mathrm{\Omega^{-1}},\qquad1\ \mathrm{M\Omega}=10^6\ \mathrm{\Omega}$$

## Capacitance and inductance

$$C=\frac{Q}{V},\qquad E_C=\frac12CV^2$$

$$V_L=L\frac{dI}{dt},\qquad E_L=\frac12LI^2$$

$$X_C=\frac{1}{2\pi fC},\qquad X_L=2\pi fL$$

| Unit | Equivalent |
|---|---|
| farad | $1\ \mathrm F=1\ \mathrm{C/V}$ |
| henry | $1\ \mathrm H=1\ \mathrm{V\,s/A}$ |
| microfarad | $1\ \mathrm{\mu F}=10^{-6}\ \mathrm F$ |
| nanofarad | $1\ \mathrm{nF}=10^{-9}\ \mathrm F$ |
| picofarad | $1\ \mathrm{pF}=10^{-12}\ \mathrm F$ |

## AC signals

For a sine wave:

$$V_{\mathrm{rms}}=\frac{V_{\mathrm{peak}}}{\sqrt2},\qquad V_{\mathrm{pp}}=2V_{\mathrm{peak}}=2\sqrt2V_{\mathrm{rms}}$$

$$I_{\mathrm{rms}}=\frac{I_{\mathrm{peak}}}{\sqrt2}$$

$$P_{real}=V_{\mathrm{rms}}I_{\mathrm{rms}}\cos\phi$$

## Frequency and period

$$f=\frac1T,\qquad\omega=2\pi f,\qquad f=\frac{\omega}{2\pi}$$

| Frequency | Period |
|---:|---:|
| $1\ \mathrm{Hz}$ | $1\ \mathrm s$ |
| $1\ \mathrm{kHz}$ | $1\ \mathrm{ms}$ |
| $1\ \mathrm{MHz}$ | $1\ \mathrm{\mu s}$ |
| $1\ \mathrm{GHz}$ | $1\ \mathrm{ns}$ |
