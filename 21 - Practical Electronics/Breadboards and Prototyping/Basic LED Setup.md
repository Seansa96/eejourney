
Requirements: 1 LED (5mm Red pref, 3mm or 10mm work, may need adjusted resistors). 1 Resistor of at least $220\ohm$. 2 Jumpers MM or FF, doesn't matter I don't think.  1 breadboard of course

In a numbered column in the center of the breadboard, join the LED and 1 leg of the resistor electrically (put them in the same column). The LED should be joined by its positive lead. Put the **negative lead** of the LED in the negative rail. The resistor's other leg can go anywhere, just put it in a nice spot in the power rail for convention.

5V Connection goes into Positive Rail, Ground Connection goes to Negative Rail.  Connect everything but the 5V and when ready, just use it like a switch and push it in or just touch it to light the LED.
