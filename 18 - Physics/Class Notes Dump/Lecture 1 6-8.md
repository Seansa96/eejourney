
# Velocity

$$
v = \frac{\Delta x}{\Delta t}
$$

where $\Delta x$ can be imagined as $x_f - x_i$ where $f$ is the final position, and $i$ is the initial position.

Similarly, $\Delta t$ can be imagined as $t_f - t_i$.

# Acceleration

Acceleration is the rate of change of velocity, or its derivative. Thus, acceleration is the $v_f - v_i$ all over $t_f - t_i$. Through algebra, this manipulation reveals that it is the change in meters over time squared, thus the units are meters per second$^2$.
