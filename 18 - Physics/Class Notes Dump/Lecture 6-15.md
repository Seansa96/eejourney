## River and Boat


### Types of these problems

**Core Idea**

There are always two velocities.
1. Boat Relative to the water
2. Water relative to the shore

The observed motion is

$$
\vec{v}_{bs=\vec{v}_{bw}+\vec{v}_{ws}}
$$
1. Upstream/Downstream
2. Cross River and Drift
3. Land Directly Opposite

### Strategy

Aim upstream.

The boat's upstream component cancels the current.

$$
v_{boatx}=v_{current}
$$
1. Draw right triangle
2. Current is one leg
3. Boat speed is hypotenuse
4. Solve angle with trig

Ask:

Are they asking where I land?
Are they asking how to land directly opposite?
