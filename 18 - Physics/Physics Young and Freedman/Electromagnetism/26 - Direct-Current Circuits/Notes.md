## Time Constant

$$
\tau=RC
$$

After some time equal to RC, the current in the RC Circuit decreases to $1/e$ which is about $0.368$ of its initial value. The capacitor charge correspondingly is then at $(1-1/e)$ of its final value, being about $0.632$. So, this product is a measure of how quickly hte capacitor charges. It's called the **time constant** or the **relaxation time** of the circuit.

When $\tau$ is small, the capacitor charges quickly, and thus when it is larger the charging will take more time. If resistance is small, then the time constant is small, so charge is faster (easier for current to flow).  Current will never actually be $0$, but the longer it goes the closer it will get.
