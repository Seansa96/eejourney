The process of losing or gaining an electron, typically caused by the impact of another electron knocking an atom's valence electron out of orbit.

The more valence electrons, the harder it is to ionize a material. 

# Ionizing Radiation

Radiation that causes ionization is called ionizing radiation. The frequency band that causes this is from $2.4 *10^6$GHz and above, or $2.4$PHz..  This threshhold sits at the higher end of the UV band, called **Extreme Ultraviolet**, and continues up through **Gamma rays**.
