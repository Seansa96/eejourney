# GSM

The Global System for Mobile Communications (also called Groupe Special Mobile) was the standard/protocol that defined 2G networks. Developed in Europe by the European Telecommunications Standards Institute ([[ETSI]]), it was made to replace 1G networks and came to dominate the majority of cellular market share during its time from the early 90s to early 2010s.  It leveraged such protocols and technology such as [[Time Division Multiple Access]], circuit-switching then packet switching, via the General Packet Radio Service (GRPS) and Enhanced Data Rates for GSM Evolution (EDGE), which from what I remember from [[A Brief History of Everything Wireless by Petri Launiainen]], is indeed a very "slick" name.
