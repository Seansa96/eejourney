Full Disk Encryption is just that--an entire drive is encrypted at once and relies on a single key to decrypt it and allowing access. The most popular modes of doing so are things like Bitlocker for Windows Enterprise, VeraCrypt for all platforms, and FileVault for MacOS.

These keys can be stored on an external device, such as a [[Trusted Platform Module]], or even on an encrypted partition of the drive itself in the case of a self-encrypting drive.
