Phishing is the act of sending illegitimate and/or malicious content to an individual, organization, or multitude of individuals with the intent of deceiving them, exfiltrating data, or intentionally securing payment or some monetary gain.

There have been several extensions that have developed over the years, making this more of a broad category of a particular kind of attack that combines social engineering and software and/or hardware.

## Spear Phishing

This refers to a phishing attack that targets specifically higher value targets--whether that value is for that individual's privilege level or potential financial exploitation.  These typically refer to attacks against directors, C-suite executives, or similar level individuals.  It can also refer to a particular demographic, such as generally more affluent targets.

## Smishing

This is a form of phishing done through text messaging, named after [[SMS]], a prevalent messaging protocol. 

## Vishing

This is a form of phishing done through voice calls or leaving voice messages directing the victim to a malicious resource.
