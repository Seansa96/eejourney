Rivest, Shamir, Adleman, referring to the creators of the algorithm. This is a public key algorithm which derives its security from the difficulty of factoring the product of two large prime numbers.

This is not typically used for data encryption, and more for the transmission of shared keys for symmetric key cryptography.

Key sizes generated with this algorithm can be variable, but commonly are in the range of 2048 to 4096 bits.
