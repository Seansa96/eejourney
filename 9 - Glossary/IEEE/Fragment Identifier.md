The part of a URI that may be included if necessary to indicate the position of a resource within an HTML document.

The syntax to denote it is as such:

`http://somesite.com/html/top.html#section_2`

where "#section2" is the fragment identifier.

This may be used with the anchor tag and [[href]] attribute to link an anchor to a destination within the same resource (such as from one position on a page to a heading, for smoother navigation for the user).
