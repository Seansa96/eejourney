# Physics CIR — Remediation Protocols

---

# Score Band: 1–6

# Fragmented Physical Understanding

## Characteristics

- Equations feel disconnected from reality
    
- Heavy formula memorization dependence
    
- Difficulty identifying relevant variables
    
- Weak intuition for motion, force, energy, or fields
    
- Cannot reliably convert scenarios into models
    
- Procedural steps feel arbitrary
    

## Primary Goal

Build physical intuition and representation competency.

---

## Core Remediation Protocol

### 1. Physical Interpretation First

Before equations, ask:

- What is physically happening?
    
- What changes over time?
    
- What remains conserved?
    
- What interactions exist?
    

Do not begin with formulas.

---

### 2. Diagram Construction

Always create:

- free-body diagrams,
    
- motion diagrams,
    
- field sketches,
    
- energy flow diagrams,
    
- or geometric representations.
    

Physics understanding is heavily visualization-dependent.

---

### 3. Variable Definition Training

For every problem:

- define every variable explicitly,
    
- include units,
    
- identify knowns/unknowns,
    
- identify constants vs changing quantities.
    

---

### 4. Unit & Dimensional Awareness

Check:

- unit consistency,
    
- dimensional compatibility,
    
- physical plausibility.
    

This develops physical sanity-checking.

---

## Recommended Activities

- Diagram-only exercises
    
- Variable-identification drills
    
- Unit analysis practice
    
- Qualitative prediction questions
    
- Graph interpretation
    
- Verbal explanation before calculation
    

---

## Common Friction Patterns

|Friction|Repair|
|---|---|
|Translation|Convert word problems into diagrams first|
|Visual|Motion sketching and graphing|
|Symbolic|Algebra isolation drills|
|Abstraction|Use concrete physical examples|
|Memory|Derive equations from definitions|

---

# Score Band: 7–10

# Emerging Operational Fluency

## Characteristics

- Can solve standard textbook problems
    
- Recognizes common equation families
    
- Understands portions of the physical structure
    
- Struggles with:
    
    - multi-step modeling,
        
    - unusual setups,
        
    - or conceptual transfer
        
- Physical intuition is inconsistent
    

## Primary Goal

Develop model-selection and system-linking ability.

---

## Core Remediation Protocol

### 1. Governing Principle Identification

For every problem, identify:

- conservation law,
    
- force relationship,
    
- kinematic constraint,
    
- or field interaction  
    BEFORE solving.
    

Goal:  
Recognize the governing system, not just the equation.

---

### 2. Multi-Representation Practice

Translate between:

- words,
    
- diagrams,
    
- graphs,
    
- equations,
    
- and physical intuition.
    

This is one of the most important physics skills.

---

### 3. Constraint Recognition

Practice identifying:

- boundary conditions,
    
- assumptions,
    
- idealizations,
    
- approximations,
    
- and hidden constraints.
    

---

### 4. Mixed Problem Exposure

Avoid isolated chapter-style drilling only.

Use mixed sets requiring:

- method selection,
    
- model discrimination,
    
- and representation switching.
    

---

## Recommended Activities

- Free-body decomposition
    
- Equation-selection drills
    
- Graph-to-equation conversion
    
- Energy vs force method comparison
    
- Estimation/Fermi problems
    
- Error prediction exercises
    

---

## Common Friction Patterns

|Friction|Repair|
|---|---|
|Model Selection|Mixed-problem sets|
|Translation|Multi-representation exercises|
|Procedural Collapse|Structured solving templates|
|Weak Intuition|Prediction-before-solving|
|Graph Interpretation|Motion graph drills|

---

# Score Band: 11–14

# Integrated Physical Competency

## Characteristics

- Strong conceptual understanding
    
- Reliable standard problem-solving ability
    
- Can explain systems from first principles
    
- Understands conservation frameworks
    
- Handles moderate novelty successfully
    
- Still struggles with:
    
    - highly abstract systems,
        
    - edge cases,
        
    - or synthesis-heavy problems
        

## Primary Goal

Increase flexibility, abstraction depth, and transfer.

---

## Core Remediation Protocol

### 1. First-Principles Reconstruction

Derive:

- kinematics,
    
- conservation laws,
    
- field relationships,
    
- and wave behavior  
    from foundational assumptions.
    

Goal:  
Reduce dependence on memorized formulations.

---

### 2. Cross-System Integration

Connect:

- mechanics,
    
- electromagnetism,
    
- waves,
    
- thermodynamics,
    
- and circuits  
    through shared principles:
    
- conservation,
    
- symmetry,
    
- rates of change,
    
- fields,
    
- and interactions.
    

---

### 3. Edge-Case Analysis

Investigate:

- limiting behavior,
    
- approximation breakdown,
    
- non-ideal systems,
    
- and unusual boundary conditions.
    

---

### 4. Latency Reduction

Focus on:

- rapid model recognition,
    
- symbolic fluency,
    
- and efficient setup construction.
    

---

## Recommended Activities

- Derivation practice
    
- Novel scenario problems
    
- Conceptual explanation exercises
    
- Estimation and scaling analysis
    
- Comparative solution methods
    
- Boundary-condition analysis
    

---

## Common Friction Patterns

|Friction|Repair|
|---|---|
|Edge Cases|Non-ideal system exploration|
|Latency|Timed model-identification drills|
|Transfer Weakness|Cross-domain problem solving|
|Over-Procedural Thinking|First-principles derivation|
|Abstract System Gaps|Unified-principle mapping|

---

# Score Band: 15–16

# Generalized Physical Mastery

## Characteristics

- Strong physical intuition
    
- Rapid model-selection ability
    
- Handles novel systems effectively
    
- Can move fluidly between representations
    
- Understands deep structural connections
    
- Capable of abstraction and synthesis across domains
    

## Primary Goal

Generalization, synthesis, and refinement.

---

## Core Remediation Protocol

### 1. Unified Framework Thinking

Study physics through:

- symmetry,
    
- invariants,
    
- conservation laws,
    
- differential relationships,
    
- and field formulations.
    

Move beyond isolated domains.

---

### 2. Advanced Modeling

Engage with:

- complex systems,
    
- approximation methods,
    
- perturbation thinking,
    
- and computational approaches.
    

---

### 3. Cross-Disciplinary Integration

Connect physics to:

- mathematics,
    
- circuits,
    
- signal processing,
    
- programming,
    
- control systems,
    
- and engineering design.
    

---

### 4. Weak-Link Elimination

Identify:

- narrow conceptual blind spots,
    
- computation bottlenecks,
    
- or abstraction inconsistencies.
    

---

## Recommended Activities

- Olympiad-style physics
    
- Research-style modeling
    
- Advanced derivations
    
- Simulation/programming integration
    
- Teaching and tutoring
    
- Formal mathematical formulations
    

---

## Common Friction Patterns

| Friction                  | Repair                         |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Narrow Blind Spots        | Targeted edge-case exploration |
| Over-Abstraction          | Real-world modeling            |
| Computational Bottlenecks | Symbolic fluency training      |
| Theory Gaps               | Formal derivation study        |
| Speed                     | Timed synthesis problems       |
