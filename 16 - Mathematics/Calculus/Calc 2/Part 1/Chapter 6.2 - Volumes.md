
The volume is the limit of the Riemann sum of a definite integral.

The true definition is as follows:

**Let $S$ be a solid that lies between $x=a$ and $x=b$. If the cross sectional area of $S$ in the plane $P_{x}$ through $x$ and perpendicular to the $x$-axis, is $A(x)$, where $A$ is a continuous function, then the volume of $S$ is**

$$
V=\lim_{ n \to \infty }\sum^{n}_{i=1}A(x^*_{i})\Delta x=\int^{b}_{a}A(x)dx
$$

For a cylinder, remember that the cross-sectional area is constant. $A(x)=x$ for all $x$. 

This means that $V=\int^{b}_{a}Adx=A(b-a)$ which is in agreement with the simple definition of
$V=Ah$.
