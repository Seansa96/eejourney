## Root method for solving systems of equations

When reduced to the form

$$
x=A(x+c)+B(x+d)
$$

Let $x=$ the root of one side. This cancels that factor leaving the other, allowing you to solve for it.

### Example

$$
x=A(x+4)+B(2x-1)
$$
$$
-4=B(-9)
$$
Solve for $B$. $B=\frac{4}{9}$

Then solve for $A$ using $B$.
