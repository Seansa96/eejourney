The Product rule for derivatives states

$$
\frac{d}{dx}[f(x)g(x)]=f'(x)g(x)+g'(x)f(x)
$$

For an indefinite integrals, this becomes

$$
\int f(x)g'(x)+g(x)f'(x)
$$

or

$$
\int f'(x)g(x)+\int g'(x)f(x)
$$

Put simply, and involving substitution, this becomes:

$$
\int udv = uv-\int vdu
$$
