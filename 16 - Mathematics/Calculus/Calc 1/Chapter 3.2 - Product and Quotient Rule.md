
# The Product Rule

$$
\frac{d}{dx}[f(x)*g(x)] = f'x*g(x) + g'x*f(x)
$$

Or, in other words, the derivative of a function times another function is the derivative of the first function times the first function, plus the derivative of the second function times the second function.

# Quotient Rule

$$
\frac{d}{dx}[\frac{f(x)}{g(x)}] = \frac{f'x*g(x)-g'x*f(x)}{g(x)^2}
$$

Or, in other words, the derivative of the first function times the second function minus the derivative of the second function times the first function, all of which is over the square of the second function.

The easiest way to do this is to do the cross multiply method from the **Product Rule**, but subtract instead of add, and of course divide the difference by the square of the second function.
