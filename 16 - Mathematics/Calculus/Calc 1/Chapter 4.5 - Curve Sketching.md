For sketching curves quick, you should run through this checklist:

* Find Asymptotes
* Find intercepts
* Find signs of $f$
* Critical points
* sketch
* Refine as needed.



**Alternate Method**

* Find first derivative and its critical values
* Make a first derivative sign chart
* Find intervals of increase and decrease and local min/max
* Find second derivative and inflection points
* Second derivative sign chart
* Intervals of concavity
* Graph
*
