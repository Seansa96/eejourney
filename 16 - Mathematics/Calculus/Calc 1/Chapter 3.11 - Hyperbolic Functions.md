# Addition and Subtraction of Hyperbolic functions


$$
\cosh x+\sinh x=\frac{e^x+e^{-x}}{2}+\frac{e^x-e^{-x}}{2}
$$
which equals

$$
e^x
$$

$$
\cosh x-\sinh x=\frac{e^x+e^{-x}}{2}-\frac{e^x-e^{-x}}{2}
$$

Which equals

$$
e^{-x}
$$


# Derivatives of Inverse Hyperbolic Trig functions

![[Pasted image 20260322051709.png]]

$$
\frac{d}{dx}\sinh^{-1}x
$$
Flip the sign inside the radical.

$$
\frac{d}{dx}\tanh^{-1}x
$$
Flip the sign in the denominator

$$
\frac{d}{dx}\csc h^{-1}x
$$
Reverse the order inside the radical, flip sign outside, multiply by abs val of x


$$
\frac{d}{dx}\sech^{-1}x
$$

Flip sign outside, reverse order inside, multiply by x

$$
\frac{d}{dx}\coth^{-1}x
$$

Same as tan, just a different domain.
