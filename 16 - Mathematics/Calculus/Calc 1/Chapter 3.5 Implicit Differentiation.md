
Typically, we deal with one function explicitly defined in terms of another, such as

$$
y= 2x
$$

But, other types exist, which we have seen at times, like:

$$
x^2 + y^2 = 25
$$

In more complicated forms, these are difficult to near impossible to do by hand without the use of technology.

# Example 1

$$
x^2 +y^2 = 25
$$
Remember that for the purpose of this equation, we treat $y$ as a function of $x$.  So, taking the derivative of $y^2$ will require the chain rule, since it's a composition function.

$$
\frac{d}{dx}{x^2}
$$ is just one function, $x^2$.

$$
\frac{d}{dx}y^2
$$
Is the derivative function of $\frac{d}{dx}$ but nested inside of it is $y^2$. So
