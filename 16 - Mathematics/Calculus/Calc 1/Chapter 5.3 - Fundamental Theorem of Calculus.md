
# Part 1

Simply states that

For any given integral

$$
F(x)=\int^{a}_{x} f(t)dt
$$

then

$$
F'(x)=f(x)
$$


## Example 

$$
\frac{d}{dx}( \int^{0}_{x} 3t^2+5)dt
$$


> [!answer]- Answer
> $3x^2 +5$ 

Basically, you just replace the given variable with another dummy variable.


# Part 2

$$
\int^{a}_{b}f(x)dx=F(b)-F(a)
$$

This just states that the integral of a given function is just the difference between the antiderivative evaluated at $b$ and $a$.


## Example 

$$
\int^{3}_{0}x^2 dx
$$
