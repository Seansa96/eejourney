The Mean Value Theorem states that given a differentiable (and thus continuous) closed interval, there exists some number $c$ where $f'(x)$ is equal to the slope of the overall interval.

This can be represented with the following equation.

$$
f'(c)=\frac{f(b)-f(a)}{b-a}
$$

# Example 1


Consider

$$
f(x)=x^3-x, a=0, b=2
$$
Find a number $c$  such that 

$$
f(b)-f(a)=f'(c)(b-a)
$$
