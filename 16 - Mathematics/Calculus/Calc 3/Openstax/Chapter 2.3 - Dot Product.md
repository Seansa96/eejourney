## Vector Projections

For vectors $\mathbf u$ and $\mathbf v$ with $\mathbf v\ne\mathbf0$:

- **Scalar projection** (the signed length of $\mathbf u$ in the direction of $\mathbf v$):

  $$\operatorname{comp}_{\mathbf v}\mathbf u=\frac{\mathbf u\cdot\mathbf v}{\lVert\mathbf v\rVert}$$

- **Vector projection** (the actual vector parallel to $\mathbf v$):

  $$\operatorname{proj}_{\mathbf v}\mathbf u=\frac{\mathbf u\cdot\mathbf v}{\lVert\mathbf v\rVert^2}\mathbf v
  =\frac{\mathbf u\cdot\mathbf v}{\mathbf v\cdot\mathbf v}\mathbf v$$

The remaining perpendicular component is

$$\mathbf u-\operatorname{proj}_{\mathbf v}\mathbf u.$$

> Read $\operatorname{proj}_{\mathbf v}\mathbf u$ as “the projection of $\mathbf u$ **onto** $\mathbf v$.” The denominator and final multiplication use the vector being projected **onto**.
