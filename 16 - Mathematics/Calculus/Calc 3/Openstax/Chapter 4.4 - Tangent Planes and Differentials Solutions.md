# Problem 196

$$\boxed{dV=2\pi rh\,dr+\pi r^2\,dh.}$$

The first term estimates volume added by changing the radius; the second estimates volume added by changing the height.

# Problem 197

Use $r=4$, $h=12$, $dr=0.04$, and $dh=0.08$ (both end caps):

$$dV=2\pi(4)(12)(0.04)+\pi(4)^2(0.08).$$

Thus the estimated aluminum volume is

$$\boxed{5.12\pi\text{ cm}^3\approx16.1\text{ cm}^3.}$$

# Problem 198

At $(1,1)$, $z=\sqrt2$, $z_x=z_y=-1/\sqrt2$. With $dx=0.01$, $dy=-0.03$,

$$\boxed{dz=\frac{0.02}{\sqrt2}\approx0.01414.}$$

The exact change is $\sqrt{2.039}-\sqrt2\approx0.01373$.
