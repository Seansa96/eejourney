

For an oblique triangle

$$
\sin a = \frac{h}{b}; h=b\sin a
$$

For a right triangle,

$$
\sin b=\frac{h}{a};h=a\sin b
$$


Equating these in terms of $h$ gives:

$$
b\sin a=a\sin b
$$

This gives us the **Law of Sines** identity, represented thusly:

$$
\frac{\sin a}{a}=\frac{\sin b}{b} = \frac{\sin c}{c}
$$


To apply this to a specific triangle, we must know the values of 3 of the 4 variables. These conditions can be thought of as:

1. Two sides and an angle opposite one of them (SSA)
2. Two angles and any side (AAS or ASA)
