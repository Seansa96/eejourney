# The Six Trig Functions

$$
sin\theta = \frac{y}{r}, cos\theta = \frac{x}{r}, tan\theta=\frac{y}{x}
$$
$$
sec\theta=\frac{r}{x}, csc\theta=\frac{r}{y}, cot\theta=\frac{x}{y}
$$

Because $r$ cannot be zero, as it is a distance, then sine and cosine are defined for any real value of $\theta$.
