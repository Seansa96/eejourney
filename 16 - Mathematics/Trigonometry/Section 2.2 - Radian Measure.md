
# Converting

To convert from degrees to Radians, it's $$Angle*\frac{\pi}{180}$$

To convert from Radian to degrees, it's 

$$
Radians * \frac{180}{\pi radians}
$$


# Deriving radian values for Q2, Q3, Q4


| Q2           | Q3           | Q4            |
| ------------ | ------------ | ------------- |
| $\pi+\theta$ | $\pi-\theta$ | $2\pi-\theta$ |
