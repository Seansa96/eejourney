Formally, this is the value 

$$
D=b^2-4ac
$$
This is the value under the radical in the quadratic formula.  It is known as a "filter" for quadratic equations, as it reveals the number and nature of the roots (which is synonymous with the solutions of an equation).


# Three Cases

$$
D>0
$$
Means that there are two distinct real roots, so the graph will cross the x-axis twice.

$$
D=0
$$
Means there is one repeated real root, and graphically it will only touch the x-axis at one point, the vertex.

$$
D<0
$$
Means there are two complex conjugate roots, but no real solutions and so the graph will not touch or cross the x-axis.
