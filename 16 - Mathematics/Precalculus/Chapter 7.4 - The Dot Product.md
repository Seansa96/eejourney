

# Definition of the Dot Product

Let $a=$ $(a_{1},a_{2})=a_{1}i+a_{2}j$ and $b=(b_{1}b_{2})b_{1}i+b_{2}j$.

The dot product of $a$ and $b$, denoted $a*b$, is

$$
a*b=(a_{1},a_{2})*(b_{1},b_{2})=a_{1}b_{1}+a_2b_{2}
$$

We also refer to the dot product as the scalar product or the inner product. 

## Example 1 - Finding the Dot Product of Two Vectors

a) $a=(-5,3), b=(2,6)$

b) $a=4i+6j, b=3i-7j$
