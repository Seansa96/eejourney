# Definition of Geometric Sequence

A sequence $a_1,a_1,...,a_n,...$ is a geometric sequence if $a_1\neq0$, and if there is a real number $r \neq 0$ such that for every positive integer $k$.

$$
a_{1+1}=a_{1}r
$$
The number $r=\frac{a_{k+1}}{a_{k}}$ is called the common ratio of the sequence. Note that the common ratio $r=a_{k+1}/a_k$ is the ratio of any two successive terms of a geometric sequence.


# Formula for the *nth* term of a Geometric Sequence


$$
a_{n}=a_{1}r^{n-1}
$$
or in general

$$
a_{n=a_{k}r^{n-1}}
$$
where $n$ and $k$ are positive integers and $r$ is the common ratio.

# Theorem: Formula for $S_n$


The *nth* partial sum $S_n$ of a geometric sequence with first term $a_1$ and common ratio $r \neq 1$ is 

$$
S_{n}=a_{1}\frac{1-r^n}{1-r}
$$

# Theorem on the Sum of an Infinite Geometric Series

If $|r| < 1$, then the infinite geometric series has the sum 
$$
S=\frac{a_{1}}{1-r}
$$
This implies that as we add more terms of the indicated infinite geometric series, the sum gets closer to $$\frac{a_1}{1-r}$$
