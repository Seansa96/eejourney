# Asked for probability

Trigger: Asked for Probability in the form $P(A)$ or similar.
Action: Reduce to a decimal. This decimal is bound between $0$ and $1.0$.

# Asked for conditional probability

Trigger: Asked for the conditional probability where the values are expressly given
Action: Simply plug in values. Remember that intersection (or $\cap$ is just "and" or "both")and that it is over the probability of the first event, $A$.

# Asked for probability that an event is one even or the other

Trigger: Asked for probability of a person having both a dog or a cat
Action: Subtract the probability of the overlap, which is people who own both, from both events, then add up the new probabilities plus the old cumulative probability (chance of owning both)

Example: .32 Dog, .18 cat, .08 both. Subtract .08 from both, then add up the new values with .08.

# Given probability in a weird form

Trigger: Told that the probability was one fourth of the probability of the other event.
Action. Take 25% of the probability of that event instead of putting 25% and calling it a day.

# Finding the probability of two events both happening

Trigger: Given events A, B, and B|A, told to find $P(A\cap B)$.
Action: Use the general multiplication rule, which says that $P(A\cap B)=P(A) * P(B)$.
