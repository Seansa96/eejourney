# 🧠 The Core Goal (anchor this mentally)

You are training two abilities:

1. **Spatial Thinking**
    
    - “Can I _see_ what this equation/problem represents?”
        
2. **Construction / Reconstruction**
    
    - “Can I _build_ this result from relationships instead of recalling it?”
        

Everything in the plan feeds those two.

---

# 🏗️ Overall Structure (how Chinese-style progression works)

Chinese pedagogy tends to follow this progression:

### Phase 1 — Concrete → Visual

- Shapes, areas, rearrangements
    
- Build intuition
    

### Phase 2 — Visual → Algebraic

- Translate shapes into expressions
    

### Phase 3 — Algebraic → Structural

- Recognize patterns and transformations
    

### Phase 4 — Structural → Generalization

- Reconstruct formulas and methods

# 📅 Your Training Plan (4–6 weeks, modular)

This is a **parallel system** (you run this alongside Calc, not instead of it).

# 🔷 Phase 1 (Week 1–2): Spatial Foundations

Goal:

> Build _visual intuition_ for relationships

---

## 🔹 Daily Block (~30–45 min)

### 1. Geometric Decomposition (core exercise)

Work with:

- rectangles
    
- triangles
    
- circles
    

Do things like:

- Cut shapes into pieces mentally
    
- Rearrange them
    
- Compare areas
    

Example tasks:

- Show why:
    
    - triangle area = ½bh
        
    - without using the formula
        
- Rearrange a rectangle into a parallelogram
    
- Turn a circle into “triangle-like wedges”
    

---

### 2. Visual Identity Training

Instead of memorizing:

- ( a^2 - b^2 )
    

You:

- Draw squares
    
- Remove a smaller square
    
- Rearrange
    

Goal:

> Every identity = a picture

---

### 3. Mental Rotation / Transformation

Practice:

- Reflect shapes
    
- Rotate them
    
- Imagine slicing them
    

You can use:

- simple sketches (not precise)
    
- or just mental visualization
    

---

## 🧠 Key Habit

Ask constantly:

> “What does this _look like_?”

---

# 🔷 Phase 2 (Week 2–3): Visual → Algebra Bridge

Goal:

> Turn pictures into equations

---

## 🔹 Daily Block

### 1. Draw → Equation

Example:

- Draw a rectangle split into parts
    
- Write area as:
    
    - one whole expression
        
    - sum of parts
        

Then match them → you’ve derived an identity

---

### 2. Reverse Direction (IMPORTANT)

Given:

- an algebraic expression
    

Ask:

> “What shape could this represent?”

Example:

- ( (x+3)(x+5) )  
    → rectangle with sides ( x+3 ), ( x+5 )
    

---

### 3. Constraint Visualization

This ties directly into your calc work.

Example:

- “Perimeter fixed”  
    → imagine stretching the shape  
    → what changes? what stays constant?
    

---

## 🧠 Key Habit

> Every equation must correspond to a _structure_

---

# 🔷 Phase 3 (Week 3–5): Variation Training (this is the “Chinese core”)

Goal:

> Build pattern intuition through controlled variation

---

## 🔹 The Variation Ladder (your main tool)

Take ONE problem and create variations:

Example (area optimization):

1. Rectangle with fixed perimeter
    
2. Same but one side fixed
    
3. Same but diagonal fixed
    
4. Same but maximize something else
    

---

### Rule:

> Change ONE thing at a time

This builds:

- sensitivity to structure
    
- not just recognition
    

---

## 🔹 “What changes / what doesn’t?” drill

For every variation:

- What stayed constant?
    
- What changed?
    
- What broke?
    

---

## 🔹 Multi-representation

Solve the same problem:

- algebraically
    
- visually
    
- verbally
    

---

## 🧠 Key Habit

> Don’t solve many problems.  
> **Transform one problem many ways.**

---

# 🔷 Phase 4 (Week 5–6): Reconstruction Training

Goal:

> Build formulas and methods from scratch

---

## 🔹 Reconstruction Drills

Take things you already know and:

### 1. Re-derive them

Examples:

- Area of circle
    
- Distance formula
    
- Derivative of ( x^2 ) (via definition)
    

---

### 2. Explain without formulas

Force yourself to explain:

- why something works
    
- without symbols first
    

---

### 3. “Blank page” method

- Start from:
    
    - definitions
        
- rebuild the result
    

---

## 🔹 Constraint → Model → Solve loop

This mirrors Chinese problem solving:

1. Define relationships
    
2. Build structure
    
3. THEN compute
    

---

## 🧠 Key Habit

> If you can’t rebuild it, you don’t fully own it

---

# 🧩 Weekly Structure Example

### 4–5 days/week:

- 30–45 min sessions
    

### Each session:

1. 10 min → visual drills
    
2. 15 min → one deep problem
    
3. 10–15 min → variation or reconstruction

# 🚨 Common pitfalls (avoid these)

### ❌ Over-sketching

- You don’t need perfect diagrams
    
- You need _functional intuition_
    

---

### ❌ Jumping to algebra too early

- Sit in the visual phase longer than feels comfortable
    

---

### ❌ Treating this like normal practice

- This is NOT volume-based
    
- It’s **depth-based**
