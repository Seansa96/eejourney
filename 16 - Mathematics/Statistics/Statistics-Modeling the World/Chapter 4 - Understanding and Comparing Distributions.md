
**Boxplots** are the prime feature for comparing distributions, especially for moderate to large sample sizes. This is because they discard a lot of meaning of the distribution itself for ease of comparison.

You won't be able to easily glean at a glance the shape, skew, modality, or density, but you can compare relative values like median heights, general trends and differences in distribution, such as which has the greater outliers or is more variable.
