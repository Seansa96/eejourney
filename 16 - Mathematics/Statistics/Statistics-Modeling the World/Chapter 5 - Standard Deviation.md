
**Z-score: Calculated by taking the value, $y$ and subtracting it by the mean, then taking that over the std deviation.**

$$\frac{y-mean}{std dev}$$
The z-score is a good way to get an idea of how a particular quantitative data point compares to other quantitative points across types.  Its definition is a **measure of how many standard deviations away a value is from the mean.**
