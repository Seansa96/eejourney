# Similar bases

In order to solve for x, bases must be similar.

so,

$$3^{x+2} = 9^{2x-3}$$
must first be simplified.

We can do

$$(3^2)^{2x-3}$$

to equate the bases. Then, to raise the exponent further, you simply multiply the exponents $2$ and $2x-3$.

$(2x-3)*2=4x-6$

so,

$3^{x+2} = 3^{4x-6}$

We then run into one of the law of exponents, which says that **if the bases are similar, then the exponents must be equal**.

# Dissimilar Bases

When given an equation where the bases cannot be readily made similar, such as:

$$3^x = 8$$

## Base e

Given $e^x = 7$

We'll follow a similar route as above, but we'll use the natural log due to e.

So,

![[Pasted image 20260113233758.png]]

The natural log of e is 1, so we do $x * 1$ which is $x$, so

$x = ln{7}$
