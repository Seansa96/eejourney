
This is the "Where does this equal zero?" function. It is defined as

$$
x=-b \pm \frac{\sqrt{ b^{2} -4ac}}{2a}
$$

This shows us where a function will cross the x-axis, or in other words when something hits zero or "turns off".

You'll commonly see this when solving for x, for intercepts, for "When does it hit the ground" problems, or for "When height=0" questions.

# Vertex of a Parabola

You can find this with

$$
-\frac{b}{2a}
$$

This is the point of symmetry that intersects with the parabola.
