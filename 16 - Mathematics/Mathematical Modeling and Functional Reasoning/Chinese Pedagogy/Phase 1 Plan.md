### 1. Geometric Decomposition (core exercise)

Work with:

- rectangles
    
- triangles
    
- circles
    

Do things like:

- Cut shapes into pieces mentally
    
- Rearrange them
    
- Compare areas
    

Example tasks:

- Show why:
    
    - triangle area = ½bh
        
    - without using the formula
        
- Rearrange a rectangle into a parallelogram
    
- Turn a circle into “triangle-like wedges”
    

---

### 2. Visual Identity Training

Instead of memorizing:

- ( a^2 - b^2 )
    

You:

- Draw squares
    
- Remove a smaller square
    
- Rearrange
    

Goal:

> Every identity = a picture

---

### 3. Mental Rotation / Transformation

Practice:

- Reflect shapes
    
- Rotate them
    
- Imagine slicing them
    

You can use:

- simple sketches (not precise)
    
- or just mental visualization
    

---

## 🧠 Key Habit

Ask constantly:

> “What does this _look like_?”
