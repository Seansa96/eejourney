# (workbook) Differentiate the function (square root)

$$
\sqrt{3x}
$$

> [!answer]- Answer
> 
![[Pasted image 20260214123542.png]]


> [!answer]- Explanation
> 
We first turn the square root into the form $\sqrt(a) = a^{1/2}$ .  Then, also observe that $(ab)^m = a^m * b^m$.  So, $(3x)^{1/2}$ is equivalent to $3^{1/2} * x^{1/2*}$.  Then, we also observe $ax^b = bax^{x-1}$.  $a$ is 3, $b$ is $\frac{1}{2}$ and $x$ is $x$. So, we then do $\frac{1}{2} * 3^{1/2} * x^{\frac{1}{2}-1}$.
