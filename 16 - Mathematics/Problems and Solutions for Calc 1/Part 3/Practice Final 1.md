# Rocket position problem



---

## **Problem 1**

A rocket is launched vertically upward. Its position (in meters) at time ( t ) seconds is given by:

$$  
s(t) = t^3 - 6t^2 + 9t + 27
$$

Answer the following:

1. Find the velocity function ( v(t) ).
    
2. Find the acceleration function ( a(t) ).
    
3. At what time(s) is the rocket at rest?
    
4. Determine the intervals where the rocket is moving upward and downward.
    
5. Determine the intervals where the rocket is **speeding up** and **slowing down**.
    
6. What is the maximum height reached by the rocket?
    
7. What is the position of the rocket at ( t = 4 ) seconds?
