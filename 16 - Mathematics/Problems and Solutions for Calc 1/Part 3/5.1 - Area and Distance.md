# (textbook) 21. Determine a region whose area is equal to the given limit

This is just asking what function will produce the given expression.

"Determine a region" = Find a function that will produce a domain of values, aka a region.

"Whose area is equal to the given limit" = That "region"'s area, or the area under the curve, that is equal to the given limit, where the limit of a Riemann sum is just an expression of *how* to find that region's area.

$$
\lim_{ n \to \infty } \sum^{n}_{i=1} \frac{2}{n} \frac{1}{1+\left( \frac{2i}{n} \right)}
