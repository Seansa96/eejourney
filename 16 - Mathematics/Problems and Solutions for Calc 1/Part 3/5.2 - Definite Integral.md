# 3. Consider the fundamental theorem of Calculus

If $f$ is integrable on $[a,b]$ then $\int^{b}_{a}f(x)dx=\lim_{ n \to \infty }\sum^{1}_{n}f(x_{i})\Delta x$
where $\Delta x = \frac{b-a}{n},x_{i}=a+i\Delta x$.

Use the given theorem to evaluate the integral.

$$
\int^{5}_{2} (16-8x) dx
