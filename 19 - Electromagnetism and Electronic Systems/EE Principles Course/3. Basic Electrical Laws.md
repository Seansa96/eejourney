
# Ohm's Law

$$
V = I * R
$$
In most systems, the voltage is fixed, so generally speaking as resistance increases, current decreases.

Voltage is measured in volts, current in amps, resistance in Ohms.

Finding each value is relatively simple when only considering these three values.

For a circuit which has a voltage of 240V and a resistance of 40 Ohms, the current would be...?

$$
240 / 40 = 6 Amps
$$

# Kirchoff's Current Law

The sum of the currents flowing into a node is equal to the sum of the currents flowing out. This is a measure of the health of an electrical system.  If the values are not equal, there must be some loss within the circuit.

$$
Iin = Iout
$$
