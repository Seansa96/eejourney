# How to overlay a green screened clip in premiere pro

Easiest way seems to be go to effects (shift 7), then drag an Ultra key onto the clip you want to overlay. Make sure the clip is **above*** the clip you want it to overlay over. You may need to unlock the video tracks to do this (might be unshaded by default). 

Example: If the green screen is on track V2, background clip needs to be on V1

On effects control (shift 5), go to the Ultra key and hit the eyedropper tool, then just click any part of the green background and it should phase it out.
