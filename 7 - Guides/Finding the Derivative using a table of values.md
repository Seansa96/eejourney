1. First, clear L1 and L2. You can do this by hitting **STAT**, then **4(ClrList)**, then **2nd L1** and **2nd L2** individually.
2. Next, make sure there's nothing in **Y=**.  
3. Go to **2nd->Y=** to go to **STAT PLOT**.
4. Turn on **PLOT 1**, change the type to the second one so you can see a basic line/curve along the points, change the mark as desired, and quit out of that.
5. Go to **ZOOM**, hit **9** for **ZoomStat** (better fit than **ZoomFit**).
6. Now **Graph** to see the approximate graph of the function.
7. From the graph, determine what kind of expression this could be.  **Linear** would be a straight-ish line, quadratic would be a parabola, exponential would be a steep curve, square root or log would be a fast rising curve into a flattish line.
8. Now, go to **STAT**->**Calc**.  Pick **LinReg** for **Linear**, **QuadReg** for **Quadratic**, **ExpReg** for **Exponential**, **PwrReg** for **Square Root**, **LnReg** for logarithmic.
9. Take the **a** and **b** values and fit them into the $y=mx + b$ form, then plug that function into **Y=, Y1**. Then, differentiate it.
10. **Graph** the function and obtain the list of points.
